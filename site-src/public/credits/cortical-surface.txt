Cortical surface illustration

The three views are original renderings of the public FreeSurfer fsaverage5 pial surface distributed by Nilearn. No participant or patient data were used. The surface color is illustrative.

Source meshes: https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/main/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
Template documentation: https://nilearn.github.io/stable/modules/generated/nilearn.datasets.load_fsaverage.html
