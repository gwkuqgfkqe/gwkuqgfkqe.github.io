01 / ABOUT

Building useful interfaces. Asking deeper questions.

I am a master’s student in Biomedical Engineering at Brown University and a Graduate Student Researcher with BrainGate, advised by John Simeral. I also work with Todd Herrington at Massachusetts General Hospital on human intracranial recordings and behavior.

My research connects neural decoding, representation learning and dynamical modeling. I am especially interested in reliable human intracortical BCIs, longitudinal changes in neural population activity, and what decoding analyses can tell us about the brain.

With a background in computer science, I build models around the structure of the problem and evaluate them with explicit data and temporal boundaries.
