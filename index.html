Zonghan Du

I’m a master’s student in Biomedical Engineering at Brown University. I work with John Simeral at BrainGate and with Todd Herrington at Massachusetts General Hospital.

I study how brain–computer interfaces can recognize intended movements over time. At BrainGate, I develop decoders for hand gestures recorded across sessions. At MGH, I study how intracranial activity relates to approach–avoidance decisions.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Sun Yat-sen University.
