NeuroScribe: generating trajectories from EEG

NeuroScribe couples an EEG encoder to a movement-primitive decoder that predicts the parameters of a trajectory.

Approach and results

I trained the STFEnc encoder and an Auto-DMP decoder to estimate basis-function weights, goals, and scaling factors. NeuroScribe achieved the lowest mean squared error among the compared methods on SMR-BCI (3.42) and SignEEG v1.0 (9.24), with mean squared jerk of 0.82 and 1.95, respectively.
