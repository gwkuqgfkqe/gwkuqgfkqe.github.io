BrainGate · Brown University

Aligning finger movements 13 months apart

We used flow matching to align motor cortical recordings from two sessions covering the same 31 individuated finger gestures.

Recordings

The two sessions from one BrainGate2 participant were separated by 13 months and shared 541 neural features. We learned a continuous nonlinear velocity field that transported neural activity from one session into the representation space of the other.

Evaluation

Nearest-centroid classification of trial-averaged activity reached 98.4% after transport into the reference session and 97.7% in the reverse direction. I evaluated the alignment against CCA and linear interpolation and examined gesture-specific velocity coherence and within- versus between-class distances.
