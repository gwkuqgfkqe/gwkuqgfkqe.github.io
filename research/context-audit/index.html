What does motor cortex add beyond task geometry?

I measured how much neural signals add to finger-velocity predictions once the target, starting position, and elapsed time are known.

Finger velocity

Across 38 LINK sessions in one macaque, adding motor cortical spiking-band power improved held-out predictions in every session. Including the preceding 100 ms of measured finger position reduced the gain in every session. Across 20 prespecified confirmation sessions, the mean reduction in squared prediction error fell from 4.63% to 2.05%.

Trial pairing and reaching

Replacing neural residuals with residuals from matched trials increased error in all 20 confirmation sessions. In a separate continuous-reaching analysis involving two other macaques, recent cursor position also reduced the benefit of neural input in the prespecified linear models.
