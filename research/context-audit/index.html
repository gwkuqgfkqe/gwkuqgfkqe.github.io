What does a decoder learn?

A context-conditional audit of neural predictive gain.

Tongji UniversityUnder review · Journal of Neural Engineering

How much predictive gain is specific to neural–behavioral trial pairing after accounting for task context and measured output history?

Why this question matters

Accurate behavioral prediction can reflect task geometry, timing or output history as well as trial-specific neural information. Explicit conditioning makes these explanations testable.

My contributions

  • Developed an audit based on context-residualized neural history.
  • Separated 18 development sessions from 20 prespecified confirmation sessions in the public LINK dataset.
  • Used nested whole-trial cross-validation and neural–behavioral trial replacement to test pairing-dependent gain.
  • Compared task-dependent residual gains in MC_Maze and MC_RTT.

Results & evaluation

All 20 confirmation sessions showed positive geometry-conditional neural gains and stronger prediction with correct pairing than trial replacement. On Neural Latents Benchmark datasets, residual gains were task dependent.

Scope of the evidence

Interpretation of adjusted gain depends on the conditioning variables and task.
