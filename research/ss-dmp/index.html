Decoding movement through dynamics

I studied whether a neural decoder could predict a small set of movement controls and use them to generate smooth trajectories.

Model

I combined a convolutional Transformer with a state-space dynamic movement primitive (SS-DMP). The encoder inferred movement controls from neural history. A subject-specific prior was estimated from training kinematics.

Benchmarks

On ECoG and multi-session spiking benchmarks, the model achieved best or tied-best decoding accuracy and produced smoother trajectories. R² was 0.44 on M2 (RMSE 0.70, MSJ 1.62) and 0.42 on MouseTrack (RMSE 0.72). Performance degraded more slowly on chronologically held-out sessions than for the Transformer, LSTM, and Kalman baselines.
