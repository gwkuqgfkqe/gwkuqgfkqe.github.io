Decisions from sEEG

Within-participant decoding of approach–avoidance behavior.

Massachusetts General HospitalOngoing research

How does human intracranial activity relate to approach–avoidance decisions?

Why this question matters

Within-participant analysis can characterize the relationship between human intracranial activity and decisions while respecting large differences across individual recordings.

My contributions

  • Built a reproducible spectral-power pipeline with time-averaged, time-resolved and temporal-generalization analyses.
  • Benchmarked classical machine learning and neural networks with matched trial-level cross-validation and training-only preprocessing.
  • Designed nested feature selection, paired comparisons and permutation tests.

Current progress

The analysis includes time-averaged, time-resolved and temporal-generalization approaches, nested feature selection, paired comparisons and permutation testing.

Next questions

Regional and frequency contributions are being examined using ablation and held-out analyses.

Scope of the evidence

Regional and frequency contributions are being investigated. This page contains a high-level project description only.
