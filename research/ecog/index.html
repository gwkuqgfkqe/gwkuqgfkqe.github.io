State-space gesture decoding

Probabilistic motor-phase and finger-trajectory modeling.

Tsinghua Laboratory of Neural EngineeringResearch output · EMBC 2025

Can a decoder combine motor-phase structure with continuous finger-trajectory prediction?

Why this question matters

Hand movement has temporal phases as well as continuous trajectories. Modeling both creates a structured route from neural activity to movement prediction.

My contributions

  • Designed a hidden Markov model for rest, flexion, hold and extension phases.
  • Implemented state-specific linear dynamical systems for finger-trajectory prediction across six gestures.
  • Built probabilistic fusion that weights trajectory estimates by motor-phase probabilities.

Results & evaluation

A probabilistic fusion mechanism weights state-specific predictions with HMM output probabilities. Reported MSE was 0.068 versus 0.166 for the RNN baseline.

Scope of the evidence

The comparison applies to the evaluated task and protocol.
