State-space decoding of hand movements

I modeled movement phase and finger trajectories from epidural ECoG using a hidden Markov model and state-specific dynamical systems.

Decoder

I modeled four motor phases (rest, flexion, hold, and extension) and used the estimated phase probabilities to combine linear dynamical systems for trajectory prediction across six gestures. Mean squared error was 0.068, compared with 0.167 for the RNN baseline.

Related work

I also developed a cross-modal framework for wrist movement and ECoG, using a recurrent encoder and contrastive learning to align neural and kinematic representations.
