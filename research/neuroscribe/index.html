Dynamics-informed decoding

Decoding by dynamics

Inferring movement controls under behavior-derived priors.

Research with Zhongyuan LaiManuscript under review

Can explicit movement dynamics make neural decoding more stable and produce coherent trajectories?

Why this question matters

Unconstrained sequence regression can turn small decoding errors into inconsistent motion. A dynamical output model limits trajectories to a structured family while allowing the neural encoder to infer movement controls.

My contributions

  • Reframed continuous decoding as low-dimensional control inference under an explicit dynamical prior.
  • Built a Conv-Transformer encoder with a differentiable state-space dynamic movement primitive rollout.
  • Estimated a subject-specific prior exclusively from training kinematics.
  • Evaluated ECoG and multi-session spiking benchmarks, including chronologically held-out sessions.

Results & evaluation

Evaluation includes ECoG and multi-session spiking benchmarks. Reported M2 results: R² = 0.44 and RMSE = 0.70; MouseTrack: R² = 0.42 and RMSE = 0.72. Chronological holdouts assess cross-session degradation.

Scope of the evidence

The manuscript is under review. Model performance does not by itself establish a biological mechanism.

Earlier public version

The July 2026 preprint is titled Decoding by Dynamics: Reframing Neural Decoding as Stable Control Inference with Behavior Priors, by Zonghan Du; Lai Zhong Yuan; Liang Hu. It precedes the current manuscript listed in Publications.
